| Plan | Projects | Storage | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2 | 20 MB per project (40 MB across 2 projects) | $0 |
| Premium | 10 | 100 MB per project (1 GB across 10 projects) | $20/month |

Team plans add up to 20 seats and a shared team project with 250 MB of storage: see [pricing](https://usemycontext.ai/pricing).

Every plan, Free included, can copy in files from Dropbox. Premium, and members of a paid Team, can also copy in files from Google Drive, kDrive and Notion. Every plan uses the same server, the same [thirteen tools](https://usemycontext.ai/docs/tools), and the same [security model](https://usemycontext.ai/docs/security).
